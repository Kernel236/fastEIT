    fastEIT - Preprocessing Layer¶

    The preprocessing layer (layer 2) takes one parsed recording with pixels (ReconstructedFrameData: a Dräger .bin or a Timpel export) and returns an EITRecording ready for breath-by-breath analysis. A Dräger .eit (raw measurements) needs a reconstruction first; a Dräger .asc has no images. It is the same for every protocol (tidal breathing, PEEP trial): each breath gets its EIT timings and quality, and its ventilator values (PEEP, plateau, driving pressure, tidal volume). The segmentation by protocol and the clinical indices (EELI, TIV, GI, CoV, compliance) come in the next layers.

    The evidence for every choice is in notebooks/05_preprocessing_choices.ipynb (11 real recordings); for the pressures of each breath, in notebooks/06_peep_trial_segmentation.ipynb (four PEEP trials). The EIT steps reimplement eitprocessing v1.8.8 (Somhorst et al., JOSS 2026;11(117):8179, DOI: 10.21105/joss.08179) and the tests check them against it.

    1. Entry point¶

    from fasteit.config import PreprocessingConfig
from fasteit.models import RecordingContext, Segment
from fasteit.parsers.loader import load_data
from fasteit.preprocessing import preprocess

raw = load_data("patient01.bin")
recording = preprocess(
    raw,                                             # never modified
    config=PreprocessingConfig(heart_rate_bpm=85),   # the choices of a study, with their source
    exclusions=[Segment(120, 180, label="cough")],   # parts chosen by the user
    context=RecordingContext(
        patient_id="PZ01",
        protocol="tidal",
        tidal_volume_ml=450,                         # optional: e.g. set in volume control
    ),
)

    The same inputs always give the same output, so an application only needs to store the inputs (file, config, exclusions, context) and can recompute the rest. PreprocessingConfig is frozen: to change a parameter make a new one, e.g. dataclasses.replace(config, heart_rate_bpm=80). It holds the choices a study may change (heart rate, quality flags, lung mask threshold, exclusion margin); the details of the methods taken from eitprocessing or fixed by the evidence are constants of their modules, with their source.

    2. Steps¶

    # Step Module Source
    1 Time in seconds, pixels copied with the Dräger "no data" values as NaN pipeline -
    2 Device exclusions: frames with missing pixels (filled with the nearest frame), timing errors, gaps in the frame times exclusions -
    3 Respiratory rate from the Welch spectrum cardiac_filter eitprocessing RateDetection
    4 Cardiac step: MDN filter if the user gives the heart rate, otherwise a 0.1 s moving average (the heart is not removed) cardiac_filter Wisse et al., Physiol Meas 2024, DOI: 10.1088/1361-6579/ad46e3
    5 Breath detection on the sum of all pixels: valley, peak, next valley breaths eitprocessing BreathDetection
    6 Quality flags and exclusions of each breath; is_valid = no flag and not excluded breath_quality, exclusions notebook 05 §8
    7 Lung mask from the valid breaths: watershed of the TIV and amplitude maps at 15 % of the maximum TIV, islands < 10 pixels removed lung_mask eitprocessing WatershedLungspace, FilterROIBySize
    8 Regions of interest inside the mask and their signals regions notebook 05 §9
    9 Auxiliary signals (pressures, flow, volume, device breath markers): "no data" values as NaN, channels without data left out, not filtered, the same units for every vendor auxiliary -
    10 Airway pressures of each breath: PEEP, plateau, driving pressure breath_pressures notebook 06 §2, §4
    11 Inspiratory tidal volume of each breath tidal_volume -

    Breaths are flagged, never removed: the clinician decides what to use. Every exclusion is widened by 0.5 s on each side (exclusion_margin_seconds) and a breath that touches it is excluded. In the later layers a breath belongs to a phase or a PEEP step when it starts and ends inside it. The user-facing rules are in cut a recording.

    Quality flags of each breath:

    Column Rule
    flag_short, flag_long duration below 0.5 s or above 20 s
    flag_suspect_ti_ratio Ti/Ttot (from impedance) outside 0.2-0.6
    flag_amplitude_outlier amplitude below 0.5× or above 2× the median of the 21 surrounding breaths
    flag_duration_outlier duration above 2× the median of the 21 surrounding breaths
    flag_invalid_data within 0.5 s of a gross outlier of the signal

    3. Ventilator values of each breath¶

    The auxiliary signals get the same units for every vendor, once, in auxiliary:

    Quantity Unit From
    Pressures (airway, oesophageal, PEEP, plateau...) cmH2O Dräger mbar × 1.0197 (1 mbar = 100 Pa, 1 cmH2O = 98.0665 Pa); Timpel already cmH2O
    Flow L/s Dräger Medibus L/min ÷ 60; Timpel already L/s
    Volumes mL Timpel L × 1000; Dräger already mL (two Medibus values in L)
    Derived values e.g. mL/cmH2O, cmH2O/L/s Medibus compliance, resistance, elastance

    Minute volumes stay in L/min. EITRecording.aux_units gives the unit of each column.

    Then each breath gets, in the breath table, the first source available:

    Columns Source, in order of preference Channel used
    peep, plateau, driving_pressure (cmH2O) 1. airway pressure waveform: Dräger PressurePod, then Medibus or Timpel airway_pressure; 2. without a waveform, the PEEP and plateau computed by the ventilator (Medibus), median over the breath pressure_channel
    tidal_volume (mL, inspiratory) 1. volume curve: highest volume around the inspiration minus the lowest before it; 2. integral of the inspiratory flow; 3. the tidal volume computed by the ventilator (Medibus: mandatory, then spontaneous inspiratory value, then the generic "tidal volume"), median over the breath tidal_volume_channel

    The pressures are all read on the same channel, so that a constant offset of the sensor cancels in the driving pressure:

    • start of inspiration: the last minimum of the pressure before its steepest rise, searched near the EIT valley;
    • PEEP: the median pressure in the 0.2 s before it;
    • plateau: the median pressure from 0.25 to 0.05 s before the start of expiration, only after an end-inspiratory hold of at least 0.5 s. In pressure control the flat inspiratory pressure is read the same way;
    • driving pressure = plateau - PEEP. The peak pressure is never used instead.

    The tidal volume is read in a window from half an expiration before the EIT breath (but not before the middle of the expiration before) to the middle of its own expiration, so that it holds the whole inspiration even when the EIT valley falls a little after the start of the inspiratory flow, and nothing of the inspirations around it. On the flow curve only the run of inspiratory flow around its peak is integrated, so that noise and cardiac oscillations around zero flow are not counted. In volume control it is the volume delivered, the one set on the ventilator. If the flow does not rise during the EIT inspiration of most breaths (inspiration recorded as negative), the log gives a warning.

    A tidal volume typed by the user for the whole recording goes in RecordingContext.tidal_volume_ml: the volume set in volume control, or the mean on the case report form, only when it is the same for the whole recording (in pressure control it changes with PEEP: type it per step, segmentation.md). It is kept apart from the measured one: the next layers use the typed one first. A typed volume outside 100-1500 mL gives a warning (litres typed instead of mL?).

    4. Output¶

    EITRecording fields:

    Field Content
    raw, config, context the inputs
    exclusions manual and device exclusions, widened by 0.5 s and merged
    time seconds from the start of the recording
    rates, cardiac_filter respiratory rate, heart rate as entered, filter actually applied ("mdn" or "moving_average")
    pixels (N, 32, 32) float32 after the cardiac step
    image_mask pixels with a signal, wider than the lung
    detection_signal sum of all pixels: the breaths are its valleys and peaks
    global_signal sum of the pixels inside the lung mask
    breaths one row per breath: times and frames, flags, excluded, exclusion_label, is_valid, peep, plateau, driving_pressure, tidal_volume
    lung_mask, tiv_map ventilated lung and the mean TIV map it comes from
    roi_masks, roi_signals ventral, dorsal, right, left, layer_1-layer_4, four quadrants, cut on the ventilated lung (indices of a shift of ventilation use image coordinates)
    aux, aux_units auxiliary signals with data, one row per frame (e.g. airway_pressure_pod, esophageal_pressure_pod, Medibus peep, min_max_flag), and the unit of each
    pressure_channel, tidal_volume_channel the columns of aux the ventilator values of the breaths come from (None if none)
    log what each step did: checks passed, facts, warnings (section 5)

    Images are seen from the feet, like a CT slice: row 0 is ventral, column 0 is the patient's right.

    Breath frames index every signal of the same length, for example the pressure at the start and at the peak of a breath:

    breath = recording.valid_breaths.iloc[0]
paw = recording.aux.airway_pressure_pod[[breath.start_idx, breath.peak_idx]]

    The TIV image of one breath is its pixels at the peak minus at the start; tiv_map is the mean of these images over the valid breaths. To look at one breath, e.g. a suspected artefact:

    from fasteit.preprocessing.lung_mask import breath_tiv_map

image = breath_tiv_map(recording, breath.breath_id)  # (32, 32)

    5. Logs¶

    There are two kinds of record, kept apart on purpose:

    • EITRecording.log belongs to the result: it says which filter ran, what was excluded and every warning, and is saved with the data. Each message has a level: OK: for a check that passed (no missing frames, respiratory rate inside the searched range, heart rate away from it, pressure and volume read, every region with lung pixels), no prefix for a fact (counts, sources), WARNING: for a check that failed or a missing input with its consequence (e.g. no airway pressure). A missing input is warned once, where it is read. A recording with all it needs and no problem has no warning; the logs of segmentation and indices follow the same rules.
    • Technical logs go through Python logging (logger names fasteit.preprocessing.*). The library never configures logging: the script or application decides where the messages go, e.g. logging.basicConfig(level=logging.INFO) in a notebook, or stdout collected by Docker in an application.

    6. Limits¶

    • Checked against eitprocessing (946 breaths, two patients and a volunteer) and the pressures on four PEEP trials (notebook 06), not against a gold standard. Dräger PulmoVista and adults only (respiratory rate 6-60/min).
    • Breaths are timed on the impedance, not on the ventilator: asynchrony is not detected, and breaths within about 15 s of a sudden change of EELI are unreliable.
    • PEEP is read at the airway opening, without intrinsic PEEP (with intrinsic PEEP the driving pressure is overestimated); the driving pressure is that of the respiratory system, in a passive patient.
    • The plateau needs a hold of at least 0.5 s (or a flat pressure in pressure control) and is not validated against the ventilator. Holds, pressure control, Medibus and Timpel pressures and the tidal volume from the curves are tested on synthetic data only.
    • The tidal volume is the one the ventilator measures, leaks and circuit compression included.

    src/fasteit/
├── config.py                  PreprocessingConfig: the choices of a study, with their source
├── models/eit_recording.py    Segment, RecordingContext, EITRecording
└── preprocessing/
    ├── cardiac_filter.py      respiratory rate, MDN, moving average, heart rate warnings
    ├── exclusions.py          device and manual exclusions
    ├── breaths.py             breath detection
    ├── breath_quality.py      quality flags
    ├── lung_mask.py           watershed lung mask
    ├── regions.py             regions of interest
    ├── auxiliary.py           pressures, flow, volume and device markers, cleaned, common units
    ├── breath_pressures.py    PEEP and plateau of each breath
    ├── tidal_volume.py        inspiratory tidal volume of each breath
    └── pipeline.py            preprocess()
